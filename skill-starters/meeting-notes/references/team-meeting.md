# Team meeting format

## <Team> meeting · <date from the transcript, or "date not given">

**Present:** names, comma-separated

### Decisions
- One line per decision, in the past tense: "Moved the release to December 3."

### Action items
| Owner | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Verb first: "Fix the login bug" | as said, or "not set" |

### Open questions
- Questions that nobody answered. Leave out the section when there are none.

Keep the whole note under 200 words. No summary of the discussion: only decisions, actions and open questions.

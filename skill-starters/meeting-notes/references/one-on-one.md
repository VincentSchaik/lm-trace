# 1:1 format

## 1:1 · <name> and <name> · <date, or "date not given">

### Updates
- Two to four bullets: what each person reported.

### Feedback
- Feedback given, in neutral words. Leave out the section when there was none.

### Agreed next steps
- [ ] Owner: action (due)

Private: do not add names of other people who were not in the 1:1 unless the transcript names them.

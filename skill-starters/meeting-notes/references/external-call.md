# External call format

## Call with <company> · <date, or "date not given">

**Their side:** names and roles · **Our side:** names

### What they asked for
- Their needs, in their words where possible.

### What we promised
| Owner (our side) | Promise | Due |
|---|---|---|

### Follow-up email
Three sentences we can send to them: thank them, list what we promised, give the next date.

Never put prices or internal notes in the follow-up email.
